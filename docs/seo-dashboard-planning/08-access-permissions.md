# Access Permissions

## Level 1 — Content Editor
May edit content, product details, images, alt text, and FAQs.

## Level 2 — Marketing Manager
May edit SEO overrides (titles, descriptions), redirect requests, and social preview values.

## Level 3 — Owner/Admin
May edit company identity, trust data, global defaults, and approve legal content.

## Level 4 — Super Admin/Developer
Environment-dependent settings (via Env Vars), advanced canonicals.
